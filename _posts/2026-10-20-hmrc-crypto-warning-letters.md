HMRC is sending far more crypto tax warning letters. Figures reported by UHY Hacker Young show about 81,000 went out in the past 12 months, up around 25% on the 65,000 sent the year before.

<!--more-->

![About 81,000 warning letters sent to crypto holders in the past 12 months](/assets/blog/hmrc-crypto-warning-letters-2.jpg)

![Up about 25% from 65,000 the year before](/assets/blog/hmrc-crypto-warning-letters-3.jpg)

## What the figures show

- **About 81,000** letters were sent to crypto holders in the past 12 months.
- That is **up about 25%** from 65,000 the year before.

These are figures reported by UHY Hacker Young, based on Freedom of Information data. They are not official HMRC statistics.

## What the letters say

The letters say you may have underpaid your tax, or not paid it at all. They focus on Capital Gains Tax on crypto, such as selling for pounds or swapping one token for another.

![What the letters say](/assets/blog/hmrc-crypto-warning-letters-4.jpg)

## A letter is not a penalty

Receiving a letter is not a penalty in itself. But ignoring it could lead to an investigation, and a formal investigation that finds unreported gains can lead to penalties.

![Don't ignore a letter](/assets/blog/hmrc-crypto-warning-letters-5.jpg)

## What to do

1. Gather your transactions from every exchange and wallet.
2. Check that each disposal and the gain or loss on it is recorded correctly.
3. Fix any gaps or errors before your next Self Assessment return.
